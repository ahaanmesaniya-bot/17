R7 Play - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

About

R7 Play ("the app") is a free game hub published by R7 Studio. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information we collect

R7 Play does not require an account and does not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location. The app itself collects no personal data and has no ads or analytics of its own.

Stored on your device

The app saves your settings (theme, language, sound, animations), your favorite games, your recently played games, play counts and the last downloaded games list on your device only. This data is not sent to us. You can remove it any time with the Clear options in Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Internet use

The app downloads the list of games from a server hosted on GitHub and loads game pictures from image servers. Like any internet request, these services can see technical information such as your IP address and device type. We do not control these services; their own privacy policies apply.

Games

The games are web games: they run on the web and open inside the app in an embedded web view from their https web address. Links that leave the game open in your phone's browser. Some games may show ads from third-party ad networks, which may use cookies or an advertising ID under their own privacy policies. A game may use cookies or local storage to save your progress, and games made by other developers may collect information under their own policies. Please do not enter personal information inside games.

Children

R7 Play is a general audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Data is downloaded over the internet using standard connections. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, but we do not store your personal data on our servers.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available inside the app under Settings > Privacy Policy.
